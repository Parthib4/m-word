Project Proposal: Q4 Digital Workspace Initiative

Prepared by Product Operations • November 21, 2026

1. Executive Summary

Welcome to your web-based Microsoft Word-style rich text editor. This workspace combines classic desktop document ergonomics—including a multi-tab ribbon, live margin ruler, A4 paper layout, and real-time word count—with instant browser auto-save and one-click export to .doc or .html.

“Simplicity is about subtracting the obvious and adding the meaningful.” — Design Principles

2. Key Deliverables & Milestones

Use the ribbon above to format typography, switch quick styles, adjust page margins, or insert structured tables and images:

  • Rich Typography: Switch between Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman, Georgia, Verdana, and Courier New with custom point sizes and colors.
  • Structured Tables & Media: Click inside the table below to access contextual row and column controls automatically.
  • Focus Mode: Hide all toolbars from the View tab for distraction-free writing.
